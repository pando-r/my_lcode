'''''The Last Dance''''' is a 2020 American [[sports film|sports]] [[television documentary]] miniseries co-produced by [[ESPN Films]] and [[Netflix]]. Directed by [[Jason Hehir]], the series revolves around [[Michael Jordan]]'s career, with particular focus on the [[1997–98 Chicago Bulls season|1997–98 season]], his final season with the [[Chicago Bulls]]. The series features exclusive footage from a film crew with an all-access pass to the Bulls, and interviews of many [[National Basketball Association|NBA]] personalities, including Jordan's teammates ([[Scottie Pippen]], [[Dennis Rodman]], [[Steve Kerr]]) and then-Bulls head coach [[Phil Jackson]].

''The Last Dance'' aired on [[ESPN]] from April 19 to May 17, 2020, in the United States, while its episodes were released on Netflix internationally the day after their American airings; beginning on May 23, two episodes were aired back-to-back on ESPN's corporate partner [[American Broadcasting Company|ABC]]. [[ESPN2]] aired an alternate version of the series intended for family viewing, which removed most of the profanity heard in the episodes. The series became available on Netflix in the United States on July 19, 2020.

The series was met with critical acclaim, with praise for its directing and editing, and also for the timing of the release—during the initial weeks of [[quarantine]] during the [[COVID-19 pandemic]] when viewers were struggling to find entertainment.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/28973557/the-last-dance-updates-untold-story-michael-jordan-chicago-bulls%3fplatform=amp| title = 'The Last Dance' - The untold story of Michael Jordan's Chicago Bulls| date = May 19, 2020| access-date = August 3, 2020| archive-date = August 12, 2020| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20200812204713/https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/28973557/the-last-dance-updates-untold-story-michael-jordan-chicago-bulls?platform=amp| url-status = live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web| url = https://time.com/5820473/michael-jordan-the-last-dance-espn/| title = ESPN's New Michael Jordan Documentary Is Exactly What We Need Right Now. Here's How They Made It| date = April 14, 2020| access-date = January 2, 2024| archive-date = February 23, 2024| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20240223013813/https://time.com/5820473/michael-jordan-the-last-dance-espn/| url-status = live}}</ref> ''The Last Dance'' won the [[Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Documentary or Nonfiction Series]] at the [[72nd Primetime Emmy Awards]].

However, ''The Last Dance'' received heavy criticism from many of Jordan’s former Bulls teammates, who disputed the series's accuracy and focus on Jordan. Much of the hostility stemmed from the expectation that the documentary would center exclusively on the 1997–98 Bulls season rather than be an account of Jordan's life and career. The series's creators were also accused of portraying multiple key players of that era in an unfairly negative fashion while being excessively deferential to Jordan.<ref name="insider.com">{{cite web |url=https://www.insider.com/michael-jordan-ex-bulls-teammates-unhappy-with-the-last-dance-2020-5/| title ='The Last Dance' is over, and now Michael Jordan's ex-teammates are lining up to bash the documentary| website =[[Insider.com]]}}</ref>

==Production and release==
''The Last Dance'' features interviews and never-released footage from the [[1997–98 Chicago Bulls season]]. Over 500 hours of all-access footage was filmed and used to create the 10-part documentary series. According to [[Adam Silver]] (now NBA Commissioner, but then the head of NBA Entertainment), Jordan allowed the filming with the agreement that the footage would only be used with his direct permission. After many years, and many refusals from Jordan, he agreed in 2016 to a documentary proposal from Mike Tollin.<ref name="shelburne">{{cite web |title=An all-access Michael Jordan documentary? How 'The Last Dance' was made possible |url=http://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/29044827/an-all-access-michael-jordan-documentary-how-last-dance-was-made-possible |first=Ramona |last=Shelburne |date=April 18, 2020|access-date=April 20, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200420181610/https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/29044827/an-all-access-michael-jordan-documentary-how-last-dance-was-made-possible|archive-date=April 20, 2020|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[ESPN]] and [[Netflix]] announced their joint production of the 10-part documentary series in May 2018,<ref name="espnpressrelease" /> with the official teaser trailer being released on December 25, 2018.<ref name="complex2018">{{cite news |last1=Espinosa |first1=Joshua |title=Here's the Thrilling New Trailer for ESPN's Michael Jordan Documentary 'The Last Dance' |url=https://www.complex.com/sports/2018/12/michael-jordan-last-dance-documentary-trailer |access-date=January 4, 2020 |publisher=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] |date=December 25, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181227131640/https://www.complex.com/sports/2018/12/michael-jordan-last-dance-documentary-trailer |archive-date=December 27, 2018 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="espnfirsttrailer">{{cite tweet |user=ESPNNBA |number=1077434223689113600 |date=December 25, 2018 |title=Cannot. Wait.}}</ref> The release date was pushed back to June 2020 after another trailer was released in December 2019.<ref name="complex2019">{{cite news |last1=Tigg |first1=FNR |title=Latest Trailer for Michael Jordan Doc 'The Last Dance' Features Barack Obama, Nas and More |url=https://www.complex.com/sports/2019/12/michael-jordan-documentary-the-last-dance-trailer |access-date=January 4, 2020 |publisher=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] |date=December 24, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191225174301/https://www.complex.com/sports/2019/12/michael-jordan-documentary-the-last-dance-trailer |archive-date=December 25, 2019 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="maxim">{{cite news |title=ESPN Documentary 'The Last Dance' About Michael Jordan and the Chicago Bulls, Gets a New Trailer |url=https://www.maxim.com/sports/the-last-dance-michael-jackson-bulls-documentary-2019-12 |access-date=January 4, 2020 |publisher=[[Maxim (magazine)|Maxim]] |date=December 24, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191228080305/https://www.maxim.com/sports/the-last-dance-michael-jackson-bulls-documentary-2019-12 |archive-date=December 28, 2019 |url-status=live}}</ref> However, because of the impact of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]], ESPN released the final trailer on March 31, 2020 and moved the premiere to April 19, 2020, releasing the following statement:<ref name="NBA.com">{{cite web |title='The Last Dance' documentary set to air April 19 on ESPN |url=https://www.nba.com/article/2020/03/31/last-dance-chicago-bulls-release-date-moved |access-date=March 31, 2020 |website=[[NBA.com]] |date=March 31, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200331131255/https://www.nba.com/article/2020/03/31/last-dance-chicago-bulls-release-date-moved |archive-date=March 31, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref>

{{Blockquote|As society navigates this time without live sports, viewers are still looking to the sports world to escape and enjoy a collective experience. We've heard the calls from fans asking us to move up the release date for this series, and we’re happy to announce that we’ve been able to accelerate the production schedule to do just that. This project celebrates one of the greatest players and dynasties ever, and we hope it can serve as a unifying entertainment experience to fill the role that sports often play in our lives, telling a story that will captivate everyone, not just sports fans.}}

The series was released weekly from April 19, 2020 to May 17, 2020, with two episodes airing each Sunday.<ref name="NBA.com" /> Outside of the United States, the episodes were released on [[Netflix]] the day after their ESPN airing.<ref name="NBA.com" /> [[ESPN2]] aired a censored version of the documentary alongside the ESPN broadcast.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/espn-last-dance-michael-jordan-documentary-coronavirus-1234581163/ |title=ESPN Plans Two Versions of Much-Anticipated Michael Jordan Documentary (EXCLUSIVE) |last=Steinberg |first=Brian |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |date=April 15, 2020 |access-date=April 21, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200421034233/https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/espn-last-dance-michael-jordan-documentary-coronavirus-1234581163/|archive-date=April 21, 2020|url-status=live}}</ref> The censored version was also aired on ABC. Jordan made a $25 million donation to [[Friends of the Children]], a nonprofit organization, from funds raised based on the creation of the miniseries.<ref>{{cite news |last=Skiver |first=Kevin |title=Michael Jordan invests millions in Friends of the Children nonprofit to help break poverty cycle |url=https://www.cbssports.com/nba/news/michael-jordan-invests-millions-in-friends-of-the-children-nonprofit-to-help-break-poverty-cycle/ |work=CBS Sports |date=2 November 2018}}</ref>

According to Hehir, Jordan refused to film interviews in his home to maintain privacy. The interviews were filmed in three houses, one rented by the production and two others which were homes of friends of the production.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Guerrasio |first=Jason |title=Michael Jordan refused to be filmed in his own home for 'The Last Dance' — here's where the basketball legend's interviews took place |url=https://www.insider.com/where-michael-jordan-was-interviewed-for-the-last-dance-house-2020-5 |access-date=2020-09-29 |website=Insider}}</ref> The fifth episode is dedicated to [[Kobe Bryant]], who died in a [[2020 Calabasas helicopter crash|helicopter crash]] on January 26, 2020. Bryant's interview was featured in the episode's opening.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2889791-the-last-dance-episode-5-will-be-dedicated-to-kobe-bryant |title='The Last Dance' Episode 5 Will Be Dedicated to Kobe Bryant |last=Kasabian |first=Paul |website=[[Bleacher Report]] |date=May 2, 2020 |access-date=May 7, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200502105258/https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2889791-the-last-dance-episode-5-will-be-dedicated-to-kobe-bryant |archive-date=May 2, 2020|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
On [[review aggregator]] [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the series holds an approval rating of 97% based on 62 reviews, with an average rating of 8.85/10. The website's critics consensus reads: "A compelling and comprehensive portrait of one of basketball's great teams, ''The Last Dance''{{'}}s blend of archival footage and candid interviews confirms there's nobody quite like Mike or the team he led to victory."<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_last_dance/s01 |title=The Last Dance (2020) |publisher=[[Fandango Media]] |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |access-date=June 19, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200420185453/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_last_dance/s01 |archive-date=April 20, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a weighted average, assigned the series a score of 91 out of 100 based on 12 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.metacritic.com/tv/the-last-dance |title=The Last Dance (2020) |publisher=[[CBS]] |website=[[Metacritic]] |access-date=June 19, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200420185435/https://www.metacritic.com/tv/the-last-dance |archive-date=April 20, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref>

The ''[[Chicago Sun-Times]]''{{'}}s [[Richard Roeper]] gave the series three and a half stars out of four, writing that "while some might question whether even one of the great team sports dynasties of all time merits such a lengthy treatment, if anything each episode left me wanting more. Not only were the Bulls a team for the ages, they also gave us a sports soap opera for the ages."<ref>{{cite web |last=Roeper |first=Richard |author-link=Richard Roeper |url=https://chicago.suntimes.com/2020/4/15/21220771/last-dance-review-bulls-documentary-michael-jordan-chicago-90s-dynasty |title='The Last Dance' captures the dominance and the drama of '90s Bulls dynasty |website=[[Chicago Sun-Times]] |date=April 15, 2020 |access-date=May 15, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200415065341/https://chicago.suntimes.com/2020/4/15/21220771/last-dance-review-bulls-documentary-michael-jordan-chicago-90s-dynasty |archive-date=April 15, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref> Daniel Fienberg of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' gave the series a positive review, writing that "it's a tremendously engaging, ridiculously fun assemblage of spectacular basketball footage and reasonably introspective interviews with almost everybody you'd hope to hear from on the subject."<ref>{{cite web |last=Fienberg |first=Daniel |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/last-dance-review-1290260 |title='The Last Dance': TV Review |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date=April 14, 2020 |access-date=May 15, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200415094939/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/last-dance-review-1290260 |archive-date=April 15, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref> Writing for ''[[The New York Times]]'', [[Wesley Morris]] wrote that "Hehir has this trick where any time someone says something debatable or controversial or simply worthy of running by [Michael] Jordan, he hands him an [[iPad]] and makes him watch what was said. And every time Hehir does it, Jordan turns the reaction into gold. He's an incredulous [[Zeus]] in these moments, lightning bolts falling from his toga as he laughs, zapping lesser gods."<ref>{{cite web |last=Morris |first=Wesley |author-link=Wesley Morris |url=https://www.nytimes.com/article/the-last-dance-jordan.html |title=In 'The Last Dance', Michael Jordan and the Bulls Still Dominate |website=[[The New York Times]] |date=April 19, 2020 |access-date=May 15, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200419092058/https://www.nytimes.com/article/the-last-dance-jordan.html |archive-date=April 19, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Conversely, filmmaker [[Ken Burns]] criticized Michael Jordan's involvement in the production of the series, saying that "if you are there influencing the very fact of it getting made, it means that certain aspects that you don't necessarily want in aren't going to be in [...] and that's not the way you do good journalism." Specifically, Burns pointed to Jordan's production company, Jump 23, being listed as a partner in the series.<ref>{{cite web |last=Moreau |first=Jordan |url=https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/ken-burns-michael-jordan-the-last-dance-1234594868/ |title=Ken Burns Slams Michael Jordan Doc 'Last Dance': 'Not the Way You Do Good Journalism' |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |date=April 30, 2020 |access-date=May 15, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200501030500/https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/ken-burns-michael-jordan-the-last-dance-1234594868/ |archive-date=May 1, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref> Hehir would respond to such speculations stating "I ran into zero stop signs in the editorial process. Michael was involved, but in a distant satellite-level while making it. He never came close to the editing room and only received cuts as they were about to go on air. I think Michael is above the fray when it comes to what he wants in and out.”<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://nbcsportsgrouppressbox.com/2020/05/18/kevin-harvick-on-winning-nascars-return-race-shaquille-oneal-on-the-last-dance-quotes-from-todays-lunch-talk-live-with-mike-tirico-and-the-rich-eisen-s/|title=Kevin Harvick on Winning Nascar's Return Race; Shaquille O'neal on "The Last Dance" - Quotes from Today's Lunch Talk Live with Mike Tirico and the Rich Eisen Show on NBCSN|date=May 18, 2020|access-date=May 7, 2021|archive-date=June 20, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230620125135/https://nbcsportsgrouppressbox.com/2020/05/18/kevin-harvick-on-winning-nascars-return-race-shaquille-oneal-on-the-last-dance-quotes-from-todays-lunch-talk-live-with-mike-tirico-and-the-rich-eisen-s/|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Criticism by former teammates===
''The Last Dance'' drew positive reactions from active NBA players, impressed by Jordan's accomplishments, and thankful for the series providing much-needed entertainment in the initial [[quarantine]] period of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.newsweek.com/last-dance-nba-twitter-reacts-michael-jordan-documentary-1498857 |title='The Last Dance': Best Twitter Reactions as NBA Players Praise 'Fantastic' Michael Jordan ESPN Documentary |last=Cancian |first=Dan |date=April 20, 2020 |website=[[Newsweek]] |access-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-date=June 1, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200601201201/https://www.newsweek.com/last-dance-nba-twitter-reacts-michael-jordan-documentary-1498857 |url-status=live }}</ref> However, the other [[Chicago Bulls]] players on the Last Dance team who personally experienced all of the events that took place in the docu-series publicly disagreed with much of the docu-series' version of events, and placed the blame on Jordan for molding questionable narratives.<ref name="insider.com"/>

[[Scottie Pippen]] was reportedly "wounded and disappointed" by his portrayal in the series, although he did not make any public remarks during the documentary's airing.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/29143858/dennis-rodman-defends-scottie-pippen-last-dance-criticism |title=Dennis Rodman defends Scottie Pippen from 'Last Dance' criticism |last=MacMullan |first=Jackie |date=May 7, 2020 |website=[[ESPN]] |access-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-date=June 9, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200609132656/https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/29143858/dennis-rodman-defends-scottie-pippen-last-dance-criticism |url-status=live }}</ref> Pippen later denied any rift between himself and Jordan over the documentary;<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2020/jul/28/scottie-pippen-michael-jordan-rift-denial-chicago-bulls-last-dance |title=Scottie Pippen denies The Last Dance caused a rift with Michael Jordan |date=July 28, 2020 |website=[[The Guardian]] |access-date=July 28, 2020}}</ref> however, he told Jordan he was not pleased with the docuseries and considered it to be "about Michael trying to uplift himself”.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-12-06 |title=Scottie Pippen: 'I told Michael Jordan I wasn't too pleased with The Last Dance' |url=http://www.theguardian.com/world/2020/dec/06/scottie-pippen-the-last-dance-michael-jordan-chicago-bulls-faces-of-2020 |access-date=2021-01-07 |website=the Guardian |language=en |archive-date=January 5, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210105035237/https://www.theguardian.com/world/2020/dec/06/scottie-pippen-the-last-dance-michael-jordan-chicago-bulls-faces-of-2020 |url-status=live }}</ref>

Pippen would go on to aggressively criticize Jordan in the following years. In 2021, he wrote an autobiography, ''Unguarded'', which gives his own perspective on the Bulls' dynasty. In the book, he is highly critical of Jordan, calling him selfish, hypocritical and insensitive.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2021/11/03/sports/basketball/scottie-pippen-unguarded-book-michael-jordan.html |title='Scottie Pippen Takes Aim at Michael Jordan in New Book |date=Nov 3, 2021 |website=[[The New York Times]] |access-date=December 20, 2021 |archive-date=December 15, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211215205931/https://www.nytimes.com/2021/11/03/sports/basketball/scottie-pippen-unguarded-book-michael-jordan.html |url-status=live }}</ref> In other interviews, Pippen also stated that he thought [[Kobe Bryant]] and [[LeBron James]] were greater basketball players than Jordan,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.sportscasting.com/scottie-pippen-explains-why-kobe-bryant-was-better-than-michael-jordan/ |title=Scottie Pippen Explains Why Kobe Bryant Was Better Than Michael Jordan |date=July 11, 2020 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042339/https://www.sportscasting.com/scottie-pippen-explains-why-kobe-bryant-was-better-than-michael-jordan/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine |last1=Nelson |first1=Cory |title=Former NBA Player Scottie Pippen Puts LeBron James Ahead of Michael Jordan In G.O.A.T. Debate |url=https://www.si.com/nba/heat/miami-news/former-nba-player-scottie-pippen-puts-lebron-james-ahead-of-michael-jordan-in-g-o-a-t-debate |magazine=Sports Illustrated |language=en |date=27 May 2023 |access-date=September 8, 2024 |archive-date=September 8, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240908222845/https://www.si.com/nba/heat/miami-news/former-nba-player-scottie-pippen-puts-lebron-james-ahead-of-michael-jordan-in-g-o-a-t-debate |url-status=live }}</ref> and said that when Jordan first played for the Bulls, he was a “horrible” player and had poor shot selection.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/scottie-pippen-says-michael-jordan-was-horrible-play-chicago-bulls-foc-rcna86905 |title=Scottie Pippen says Michael Jordan was 'horrible to play with' on Chicago Bulls, more focused on scoring over winning |website=[[NBC News]] |date=May 30, 2023 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042338/https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/scottie-pippen-says-michael-jordan-was-horrible-play-chicago-bulls-foc-rcna86905 |url-status=live }}</ref>
 
[[Horace Grant]] said that ''The Last Dance'' was edited to favor Jordan, remarking that the series was "entertaining, but we know [...] that about 90% of it [was] BS in terms of the realness of it"; he also denied Jordan's accusation that Grant was the source for ''[[The Jordan Rules (book)|The Jordan Rules]]'' and agreed that Pippen was portrayed unfairly.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2020/may/19/horace-grant-michael-jordan-the-last-dance-documentary-espn |title='Lie, lie, lie': Former Jordan teammate gives withering assessment of The Last Dance |date=May 19, 2020 |website=[[The Guardian]] |access-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-date=June 9, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200609132529/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2020/may/19/horace-grant-michael-jordan-the-last-dance-documentary-espn |url-status=live }}</ref>

Grant also explained on [[Bill Cartwright]]'s podcast that he initially planned to decline the interview offer, as he was not a member of the Last Dance team. However, after much persistence by the production crew, Grant finally "reluctantly" conceded to interview because he wanted to reminisce about his days as an NBA star. He then said he took an outspoken stance about his disdain for the documentary because he wanted to defend his character and did not agree with the veracity of Jordan's version of events.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPrwXEWePHw |title=The Bill Cartwright Show EP8: Horace Grant |website=[[YouTube]] |date=December 29, 2020 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042341/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPrwXEWePHw |url-status=live }}</ref> Grant then doubled down on Jordan by expressing his frustrations about Jordan and the docu-series in a lengthy phone call with [[Shannon Sharpe]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qquXZj38EbU |title=Shannon reveals what Horace Grant told him on the phone about Michael Jordan|website=[[YouTube]] |date=May 8, 2020 }}</ref>

Cartwright, an assistant coach on the Last Dance team, and also the starting center on the Bulls’ first three-peat team, was reportedly unimpressed with the series, dismissing it as pure entertainment,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6UmL8fUnjDM |title=The Final Dance - Former Bulls React to The Last Dance - BetOnline.ag |website=[[YouTube]] |date=May 21, 2020 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042340/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6UmL8fUnjDM |url-status=live }}</ref> and said that the series was "really one guy's perspective of what happened."<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.insider.com/michael-jordan-ex-bulls-teammates-unhappy-with-the-last-dance-2020-5 |title='The Last Dance' is over, and now Michael Jordan's ex-teammates are lining up to bash the documentary|website=[[Insider.com]] }}</ref> Cartwright also explained in another interview that Jordan had been framing an inaccurate image to the public, and called his play-acting “silly.”<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jvDeU5Tp0b4 |title=Former NBA player Bill Cartwright talks about what playing with Michael Jordan was really like |website=[[YouTube]] |date=May 13, 2020 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042338/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jvDeU5Tp0b4 |url-status=live }}</ref>

[[Craig Hodges]], a member of the first two championship seasons with the Bulls, said he felt disappointed about not getting an opportunity to be interviewed for the documentary, and further criticized Jordan for discussing the team's use of [[cocaine]] during the 1980s, which was also another subject that was not related to the Last Dance season. On the issue of cocaine use, Hodges remarked, "I was thinking about the brothers who [were on the team] with you who have to explain [what happened] to their families”.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/michael-jordan-s-former-teammate-slams-basketball-star-s-cocaine-n1201241 |title=Michael Jordan's former teammate slams the basketball star's 'cocaine circus' comments in 'The Last Dance' |last=Aviles |first=Gwen |date=May 6, 2020 |website=[[NBC News]] |access-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-date=June 26, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200626064435/https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/michael-jordan-s-former-teammate-slams-basketball-star-s-cocaine-n1201241 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://chicago.suntimes.com/the-last-dance/2020/5/6/21249300/craig-hodges-michael-jordan-last-dance-cocaine-comments-bulls |title=Craig Hodges blasts Michael Jordan for cocaine comments in 'The Last Dance' |date=May 6, 2020 |website=[[Chicago Sun-Times]] |access-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-date=May 20, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200520110727/https://chicago.suntimes.com/the-last-dance/2020/5/6/21249300/craig-hodges-michael-jordan-last-dance-cocaine-comments-bulls |url-status=live }}</ref>

Some expressed disappointment over the omission of starting center [[Luc Longley]] in the series, including Longley himself. In 2021, an episode of the documentary series ''[[Australian Story]]'', titled "Luc Longley and the missing chapter of ''The Last Dance''", was released with Longley's response, including interviews with ex-teammates such as Pippen and Jordan. The documentary was a success in its own right.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.abc.net.au/news/2021-08-02/luc-longleys-chicago-bulls-legacy/100256414 |title='Luc Longley's Chicago Bulls legacy |date=August 16, 2021 |website=ABC |access-date=January 13, 2022 |archive-date=January 12, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220112230831/https://www.abc.net.au/news/2021-08-02/luc-longleys-chicago-bulls-legacy/100256414 |url-status=live }}</ref>

Following the positive reception of Longley’s documentary, the Bulls organization released an additional feature documentary on a key member of the Last Dance team, [[sixth man]] [[Toni Kukoč]]. The documentary was titled “The Waiter”, paying tribute to Kukoč’s nickname acquired in his native country of [[Croatia]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lhHNMFamco |title="The Waiter" - A Toni Kukoč Story: Kukoč's role in the Last Dance and more! |website=[[YouTube]] |date=October 30, 2021 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042339/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lhHNMFamco |url-status=live }}</ref>

Others also expressed disappointment in the lack of coverage of another starter on the Last Dance team, [[point guard]] [[Ron Harper]].<ref name=nbcsportsbayarea>{{cite web | url=https://www.nbcsportsbayarea.com/nba/golden-state-warriors/steve-kerr-a-little-disappointed-by-key-absences-in-the-last-dance/1364999/ | title='Steve Kerr 'a little' disappointed by key absences in 'The Last Dance' – NBC Sports Bay Area & California | date=August 7, 2020 | access-date=January 2, 2024 | archive-date=January 2, 2024 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042338/https://www.nbcsportsbayarea.com/nba/golden-state-warriors/steve-kerr-a-little-disappointed-by-key-absences-in-the-last-dance/1364999/ | url-status=live }}</ref> Harper was only included for a few seconds, talking about his time playing for the [[Cleveland Cavaliers]] in 1989 when he expressed disagreement with his coach for not assigning him to defend Jordan during [[The Shot (Bulls–Cavaliers)|The Shot]],<ref>{{cite web |url= https://www.sportskeeda.com/basketball/news-revisited-whatever-f-k-bulls-t-ron-harper-recalls-wanting-guard-michael-jordan-the-shot-1989-playoffs |title= Ron Harper recalls wanting to guard Michael Jordan on 'The Shot' in 1989 playoffs |date= July 28, 2022 |access-date= January 2, 2024 |archive-date= January 2, 2024 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042338/https://www.sportskeeda.com/basketball/news-revisited-whatever-f-k-bulls-t-ron-harper-recalls-wanting-guard-michael-jordan-the-shot-1989-playoffs |url-status= live }}</ref> but never was shown discussing his role in the Bulls' second three-peat. Harper had started all 82 games during the Last Dance season.<ref>{{cite web |url= https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/harpero01.html |title= Ron Harper Stats, Height, Weight, Position, Draft Status and more |access-date= February 9, 2024 |archive-date= February 20, 2011 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20110220222003/http://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/harpero01.html |url-status= live }}</ref>

There was also criticism for the series' over-emphasis on [[Steve Kerr]], a minor player on the Bulls' last championship team. Those critics alleged that the reasoning behind Kerr's over-emphasis was linked to his recent success as the head coach of the [[Golden State Warriors]]. Some compared it to a scenario where ESPN did a documentary on the 1970s [[Pittsburgh Steelers]] and placed [[Tony Dungy]] (who like Kerr had a successful head coaching career but a lesser playing career) in the same company as [[Joe Greene]], [[Franco Harris]], and [[Jack Lambert (American football)|Jack Lambert]]. Kerr himself admitted that the series emphasized him more than what was needed, and also did not condone the absence of Longley and Harper in the series.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.cinemablend.com/television/2557705/why-michael-jordans-the-last-dance-was-slightly-embarrassing-for-nba-vet-steve-kerr | title=Why Michael Jordan's The Last Dance Was Slightly 'Embarrassing' for NBA Vet Steve Kerr | date=October 25, 2020 | access-date=December 31, 2022 | archive-date=December 31, 2022 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221231131154/https://www.cinemablend.com/television/2557705/why-michael-jordans-the-last-dance-was-slightly-embarrassing-for-nba-vet-steve-kerr | url-status=live }}</ref><ref name=nbcsportsbayarea/>

Some came to the defense of former Bulls general manager [[Jerry Krause]], accusing Jordan of intentionally delaying the start of the production until Krause died in 2017 in order to prevent him from being able to explain his own version of events. ''[[Sports Illustrated]]'' questioned Jordan's narrative expressed in the series, called him “cruel”, and said that Krause deserved better treatment.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.si.com/nba/2020/05/18/jerry-krause-chicago-bulls-crumbs-the-last-dance |title=Jerry Krause Deserved Better in 'The Last Dance' |date=May 18, 2020 |access-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102042338/https://www.si.com/nba/2020/05/18/jerry-krause-chicago-bulls-crumbs-the-last-dance |url-status=live }}</ref>

===US ratings===
For the [[2019–20 United States network television schedule|2019–20 television season]], the show tied for fifth among adults 18–49 in the [[Nielsen ratings]] with ''[[This Is Us]]'', averaging a 2.9 rating and 15 share. It also placed 55th in total viewership, averaging 6.71 million viewers. ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' dubbed the show's performance a "consolation prize" for ESPN given the network's struggle to find live sports.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/most-popular-tv-shows-highest-rated-2019-2020-season-masked-singer-last-dance-1234612885/ |title=100 Most-Watched TV Shows of 2019-20: Winners and Losers |last=Schneider |first=Michael |date=May 21, 2020 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |access-date=May 29, 2020 |archive-date=May 25, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200525194319/https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/most-popular-tv-shows-highest-rated-2019-2020-season-masked-singer-last-dance-1234612885/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The series also became ESPN's most-watched documentary.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/last-dance-michael-jordan-docuseries-espn-tv-ratings-1234610101/ |title='Last Dance': Michael Jordan Series Finishes as Most-Viewed ESPN Documentary Ever |last=Thorne |first=Will |date=May 18, 2020 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |access-date=May 25, 2021 |archive-date=May 26, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210526032753/https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/last-dance-michael-jordan-docuseries-espn-tv-ratings-1234610101/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
