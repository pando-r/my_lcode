# 01 · 著作与系统性表达

> 采集自：`raw/wikiquote.txt`（Wikiquote，逐条带出版物出处）、`raw/classic_quotes.txt`（NBA.com 官方引语页，2006 年存档）、`raw/hof_speech.txt`（Sam Smith 对 2009 名人堂演讲的逐段记录，NBA.com 2013 年存档）。
> 可信度：一级（本人署名作品 / 官方收录 / 现场记录）。

## 他署名的三本书

| 年份 | 书名 | 性质 |
|---|---|---|
| 1994 | *I Can't Accept Not Trying: Michael Jordan on the Pursuit of Excellence*（HarperSanFrancisco） | 短篇格言集，讲目标设定与态度。Wikiquote 引用了 p. 20/24，以及 p. 129 的一句 |
| 1998 | *For the Love of the Game: My Story* | 自传。他在这里写：**早在 1992 年夏天他就在准备退役**（见 raw/wiki_jordan.txt 第一次退役段） |
| 2005 | *Driven from Within* | 与 Mark Vancil 合著，讲球鞋设计与他自己的审美/控制欲 |

**注意一个陷阱**："I've missed more than 9,000 shots..." 这句流传最广的话**不是他书里写的**，是 Goldman & Papson 在 *Nike Culture: The Sign of the Swoosh*（1998）里记载的 Nike 广告文案（Wikiquote 明确标注出处为该书 p. 49）。这是 Nike 的产物，不是他的自述——但它被他自己反复认可，成了他公开的自我叙事。

## 他本人讲的方法论（有出处的原话）

**目标要"合理、可管理"——这是他写过的最反直觉的一段**

> I set another goal … a reasonable, manageable goal that I could realistically achieve if I worked hard enough. I approached everything step by step.
> —— 转引自 Pat Williams, *How to be like Mike* (2005)

这段推翻"乔丹靠野心驱动"的通俗印象：他描述的方法是**把目标切到"努力够得着"的尺度，然后一步一步走**。不是宏大愿景驱动，是可达性驱动。

**接受失败，不接受不尝试**

> I can accept failure. Everyone fails at something. But I can't accept not trying [no hard work]. Talent wins games, but teamwork and intelligence wins championships.
> —— *I Can't Accept Not Trying* (1994), p. 20, 24

注意这句话的内部张力：前半句是个人主义的（我可以失败），后半句是集体主义的（冠军靠团队和智慧）。他在同一页里把两件事并列，没有调和。这个张力贯穿他整个生涯（见 SKILL.md 的"张力"一节）。

**"极限和恐惧一样，常常只是幻觉"**

> Limits, like fears, are often just an illusion.
> —— 2009 名人堂演讲结尾（raw/hof_speech.txt）

这句是他 2009 年演讲的收尾，语境是他说"也许你们会看到我 50 岁还在打球"，观众笑，他说"别笑，永远别说永远"。

**享受每一分钟，不要对人生二次猜测**

> Enjoy every minute of life. Never second-guess life.
> —— 转引自 *How to be like Mike* (2005)

## 退役时的自述（1999-01-13 发布会，raw/retire_presser.txt 全文存档）

这是他自己解释"为什么离开"最完整的一次，也是理解他动机机制的核心文本。

> Mentally, I am exhausted. I don't feel I have the challenge. Physically, I feel great.

> For the sake of the mental challenges that Michael Jordan needs to drive himself to be the best basketball player he can be, I don't want to fool myself going into the situation knowing that I am not 100 percent challenged mentally.

> "I chose to walk away knowing I that I could still play the game. That's what I always wished for my career to end. That's exactly the way I wanted to end it."

> To start something and then in the middle of the season lose interest and then lose motivation, it's unfair to the people that I am working with and working for and to the fans.

**这里的关键结构**：他的退出条件不是"打不动了"，而是"没有心理挑战了"。体能够用、挑战不够 → 走。他明确说这是为了不"骗自己"。这是可迁移的决策规则，不是运动员特有的。

**关于纪录的取舍（同一场发布会）**

> No, there is not a record that I didn't get that I would have loved to have gotten. I am pretty sure people would say, 'I would have like to lead the league and score the most points in history of the game.' But, I would rather give that up to get six titles. It's a compensation there.

**关于两个三连冠的排序（同一场发布会）**

> The second three-peat, by far, is the one that you have to cherish the most because it's the hardest. I felt it was the hardest to achieve, especially when the team was dismantled to some degree and then brought back with different talent.
> …I would say that's better than the first three. Which team is better? I would think the first team is better than the second team that won three in a row, but it was definitely harder to win the second three in a row than the first three.

**他区分"更好的球队"和"更难的成就"**——这是他少数明确做出双重评价的地方：更强的是第一支，更珍贵的是第二支。他不把"最强"和"最值"混为一谈。

## 管理/组织的立场（1999 发布会 + 2009 演讲）

> I think the game itself is a lot bigger than Michael Jordan. I've been given an opportunity by people before me, to name a few, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Dr. J, Elgin Baylor, Jerry West.

> 关于和克劳斯的冲突，他的表述（1999）：
> In terms of my disagreements with the management, about some of the decision making, that's always been my opinions. It hasn't been ultimatums or twisting the arms. … you may have disagreements but that doesn't mean the focus is not there.

到了 2009 年，同一件事的说法变硬了（见 05-decisions 与 02-conversations）。

## 和先验不一样的地方

- **加深**：先验只知道 9,000 次投篮那句话。实际它出自 Nike 的宣传文案而非他的书，而他真正写下的方法论是"合理、可管理的目标 + 一步一步"，方向几乎相反。
- **新增**：他把"退出条件"定成"心理挑战消失"，并且明确说体能够用也要走。先验里完全没有这条。
- **新增**：他会区分"更强的球队"和"更难拿的冠军"，说明他不把单一维度当唯一标准。
