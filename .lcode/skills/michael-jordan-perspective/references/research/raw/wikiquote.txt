'''[[w:Michael Jordan|Michael Jeffrey Jordan]]''' (born [[February 17]], [[1963]]), also known by his initials, '''MJ''', is an American retired professional [[w:basketball|basketball]] player, [[w:businessperson|businessman]], and principal owner and chairman of the [[w:Charlotte Hornets|Charlotte Hornets]]. Jordan played 15 seasons in the [[w:National Basketball Association|National Basketball Association]] (NBA) for the [[w:Chicago Bulls|Chicago Bulls]] and [[w:Washington Wizards|Washington Wizards]]. He is widely considered the greatest basketball player of all time.
