'''23XI Racing''' (pronounced '''twenty-three eleven''') is an American professional [[auto racing]] organization that competes in the [[NASCAR Cup Series]]. It is owned and operated by [[Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame|Hall of Fame basketball]] player [[Michael Jordan]], along with current [[Joe Gibbs Racing]] driver and three-time [[Daytona 500]] winner [[Denny Hamlin]]. The team name represents a combination of the number 23 Michael Jordan wore during most of his [[NBA]] career and the number 11 which Denny Hamlin has driven his entire Cup series career.<ref name="Long" /> The organization fields three full-time entries. They are the No. 23 [[Toyota Camry]] for [[Bubba Wallace]], No. 45 Camry for [[Tyler Reddick]], and No. 67 Camry for [[Corey Heim]]. They have a technical alliance with [[Joe Gibbs Racing]].<ref name="23XI JGR">{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2020/10/30/bubba-wallace-denny-hamlin-reveal-manufacturer-technical-alliance-for-23xi-racing/ |title=Bubba Wallace, Denny Hamlin reveal manufacturer, technical alliance for 23XI Racing |website=NASCAR.com |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC |date=October 30, 2020 |access-date=October 30, 2020 |archive-date=October 30, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201030161802/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2020/10/30/bubba-wallace-denny-hamlin-reveal-manufacturer-technical-alliance-for-23xi-racing/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==History==
[[File:23XI Racing logo.png|thumb|The previous logo for 23XI Racing.]]
During the summer of 2020, rumors swirled that Michael Jordan intended on purchasing an ownership stake in [[Richard Petty Motorsports]], who fielded the only black driver in the [[NASCAR Cup Series]], [[Bubba Wallace]]. Such claims were repeatedly denied by Jordan's management team.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Barrabi |first1=Thomas |title=Michael Jordan not interested in buying into Bubba Wallace's NASCAR team, rep says |url=https://www.foxbusiness.com/sports/michael-jordan-nascar-bubba-wallace-rumor |website=FOX Business |publisher=[[Fox News|FOX News Network, LLC]] |access-date=October 6, 2020 |date=August 27, 2020 |archive-date=October 9, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201009145349/https://www.foxbusiness.com/sports/michael-jordan-nascar-bubba-wallace-rumor |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Taranto |first1=Steven |title='No truth' to rumor of Michael Jordan exploring NASCAR ownership |url=https://247sports.com/Article/Michael-Jordan-NASCAR-rumors-no-truth-Richard-Petty-Motorsports-Bubba-Wallace-150792655/ |website=247Sports |access-date=October 6, 2020 |date=August 28, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250120201445/https://247sports.com/Article/Michael-Jordan-NASCAR-rumors-no-truth-Richard-Petty-Motorsports-Bubba-Wallace-150792655/ |archive-date=January 20, 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref>

On September 8, 2021, veteran motorsports executive Steve Lauletta was named team president after serving as the interim president since the team's foundation.<ref name="Lauletta">{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2021/09/08/steve-lauletta-named-team-president-of-23xi-racing/ |title=Steve Lauletta Named Team President of 23XI Racing |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC. |date=September 8, 2021 |access-date=September 9, 2021}}</ref>

On July 12, 2022, 23XI Racing announced that two-time [[NASCAR Xfinity Series]] champion [[Tyler Reddick]] signed with the team for the 2024 season.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2022/07/12/23xi-racing-signs-tyler-reddick-for-2024/ |title=23XI Racing Signs Tyler Reddick for 2024 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC. |date=July 12, 2022 |access-date=July 13, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220712161722/https://www.jayski.com/2022/07/12/23xi-racing-signs-tyler-reddick-for-2024/ |archive-date=July 12, 2022 |url-status=live}}</ref> On July 23, 2022, Hamlin announced that starting in 2023, 23XI would have its own pit crews instead of leasing them from Joe Gibbs Racing.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2022/07/23/denny-hamlin-says-23xi-racing-will-have-their-own-pit-crews-next-season/ |title=Denny Hamlin Says 23XI Racing Will Have Their Own Pit Crews Next Season |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC. |date=July 23, 2022 |access-date=July 24, 2022}}</ref>

In May, 2024, 23XI opened its doors to their brand-new race shop and team headquarters, named "Airspeed", in Huntersville, North Carolina.<ref>{{cite web |title=Inside Airspeed, 23XI Racing's 'all-in' headquarters |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/05/30/cup-series-2024-inside-airspeed-23xi-racing-headquarters/ |website=NASCAR.com |date=May 30, 2024 |access-date=October 10, 2024}}</ref>

=== Antitrust lawsuit with Front Row Motorsports against NASCAR ===
{{main article|23XI Racing v. NASCAR}}
On October 2, 2024, it was announced that 23XI Racing, along with [[Front Row Motorsports]], filed an antitrust lawsuit against NASCAR over the terms of the updated charter agreement, as well as anti-competitive practices committed by the [[France family]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/5810445/2024/10/02/23xi-racing-front-row-nascar-lawsuit/ |title=23XI Racing, Front Row Motorsports sue NASCAR alleging monopolistic practices |newspaper=[[The New York Times]] |date=October 2, 2024 |access-date=October 2, 2024 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2024/10/03/23xi-racing-front-row-motorsports-file-lawsuit-against-nascar/ |title=23XI Racing, Front Row Motorsports file lawsuit against NASCAR UPDATE 3 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=October 3, 2024 |access-date=October 4, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241007092051/https://www.jayski.com/2024/10/03/23xi-racing-front-row-motorsports-file-lawsuit-against-nascar/ |archive-date=October 7, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref> On December 18, both teams were granted a motion of preliminary injunction, allowing them to race as chartered entries in 2025 while continuing their legal battle with NASCAR. The injunction also allowed the transfer of the two [[Stewart–Haas Racing]] charters to both teams.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2024/12/18/teams-win-preliminary-injunction-in-lawsuit-against-nascar/ |title=Teams win preliminary injunction in lawsuit against NASCAR |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC |date=December 18, 2024 |access-date=December 19, 2024}}</ref> On June 5, 2025, the U.S. Court of Appeals overturned the preliminary injunction ruling.<ref>{{Cite web |date=June 5, 2025 |title=U.S. Court of Appeals rules in favor of NASCAR |url=https://www.jayski.com/2025/06/05/u-s-court-of-appeals-rules-in-favor-of-nascar/ |access-date=June 5, 2025 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media}}</ref> On August 25, NASCAR filed a legal notice of its agreement to issue one of the charters to a [[Redaction|redacted]] entity.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2025/08/25/nascar-has-agreement-for-transfer-of-charter-involved-in-lawsuit |title=NASCAR has agreement for transfer of charter involved in lawsuit |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=August 25, 2025 |access-date=August 26, 2025}}</ref> On December 11, 2025, after eight days in court, the case was settled.<ref>{{Cite news |last1=Bianchi |first1=Jordan |last2=Gluck |first2=Jeff |date=December 11, 2025 |title=NASCAR reaches settlement with 23XI, Front Row to end year-long legal saga |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6881791/2025/12/11/nascar-settlement-23xi-michael-jordan-trial-lawsuit/ |access-date=December 11, 2025 |work=The New York Times |issn=0362-4331 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251211155940/https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6881791/2025/12/11/nascar-settlement-23xi-michael-jordan-trial-lawsuit/ |archive-date=December 11, 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Wallace finished second in his Duel and finished seventeenth at the [[2021 Daytona 500]] after being involved in a last-lap crash. At the Daytona road course he had troubles all day and finished 26th. Wallace struggled out the gate until Phoenix, which was his first strong run of the season. Wallace was running top-fifteen for most of the day before working his way into the top-ten. Following a caution and most of the field pitting, while Wallace stayed out, he restarted first. This move did not work out well for Wallace. Due to him being on old tires and the rest of the field being on new tires, Wallace was quickly cycled out of the first position, dropped back, and wound up making contact with [[Cole Custer]]. He ended up finishing 16th.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.frontstretch.com/2021/03/14/the-underdog-house-big-day-for-bubba-wallace-slips-away-at-phoenix/ |title=The Underdog House: Big Day for Bubba Wallace Slips Away at Phoenix |website=Frontstretch.com |date=March 14, 2021 |access-date=August 29, 2021}}</ref> At the [[2021 GEICO 500]] at Talladega, Wallace had a strong car the entire day. Wallace was running top-ten for a good portion of the day and ended up winning stage 2. This was Wallace's first career stage win. After an overtime restart, Wallace was cycled to the back of the pack and wound up finishing nineteenth. At Dover, Wallace finished eleventh. At Pocono, Wallace finished the first race in fourteenth, but finished the second race fifth, scoring the team's best finish and their first top-five. He finished third at the [[2021 Coke Zero Sugar 400]] at Daytona, but he was officially scored with a second place finish after [[Chris Buescher]] was disqualified post-inspection.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/results/racecenter/2021/nascar-cup-series/coke-zero-sugar-400/stn/recap/ |title=Coke Zero Sugar 400 |website=NASCAR.com |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC. |date=August 28, 2021 |access-date=August 29, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210829051134/https://www.nascar.com/results/racecenter/2021/nascar-cup-series/coke-zero-sugar-400/stn/recap/ |archive-date=August 29, 2021 |url-status=live}}</ref> On September 16, [[Bootie Barker]] was named crew chief for the remainder of the season after Wheeler was promoted to Director of Competition.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2021/09/16/mike-wheeler-named-director-of-competition-at-23xi-racing-bootie-barker-to-serve-as-crew-chief-for-rest-of-2021/ |title=Mike Wheeler named Director of Competition at 23XI Racing; Wallace began the [[2022 NASCAR Cup Series|2022 season]] by finishing in second place at the [[2022 Daytona 500]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2022/02/20/bubba-wallace-comes-just-short-of-winning-daytona-500/ |title=Bubba Wallace Comes Just Short of Winning Daytona 500 |first=Dustin |last=Albino |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC |date=February 20, 2022 |access-date=March 10, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220310094229/https://www.jayski.com/2022/02/20/bubba-wallace-comes-just-short-of-winning-daytona-500/ |archive-date=March 10, 2022 |url-status=live}}</ref> On March 29, 2022, Barker was suspended for four races due to a tire and wheel loss during the [[2022 Texas Grand Prix]] at [[Circuit of the Americas|COTA]]. Wallace started the [[2023 NASCAR Cup Series|2023 season]] with a twentieth place finish at the [[2023 Daytona 500]]. He improved his finishes enough to make the playoffs for the first time in his career.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/08/26/cup-series-race-recap-daytona-summer |title=Chris Buescher wins regular-season finale at Daytona; Wallace makes playoffs |first=Holly |last=Cain |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=August 26, 2023 |access-date=August 27, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230902194833/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/08/26/cup-series-race-recap-daytona-summer |archive-date=September 2, 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref> Wallace was eliminated from the Round of 12 at the conclusion of the [[2023 Bank of America Roval 400|Charlotte Roval race]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/10/08/results-cup-series-2023-charlotte-roval-playoffs-race-recap/ |title=AJ Allmendinger holds off William Byron for emotional Charlotte Roval victory |first=Reid |last=Spencer |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=October 8, 2023 |access-date=October 9, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230227221310/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/02/26/results-cup-series-2023-auto-club-speedway-race-recap/ |archive-date=February 27, 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref>

During the [[2024 NASCAR Cup Series|2024 season]], Wallace was fined {{USD}}50,000 for intentionally door-slamming [[Alex Bowman]] during the cool-down lap after the [[2024 Grant Park 165|Chicago street race]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/07/10/penalty-report-bubba-wallace-fined-after-post-race-incident-at-chicago/ |title=Penalty report: Bubba Wallace fined after post-race incident at Chicago |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=July 10, 2024 |access-date=July 11, 2024}}</ref> Following the [[2024 Xfinity 500|Martinsville playoff race]], the No. 23 was docked fifty owner and driver points and Wallace and the team were each fined {{USD}}100,000 for race manipulation, when Wallace faked a tire failure and slowed down to allow fellow Toyota driver [[Christopher Bell (racing driver)|Christopher Bell]] to pass him in an attempt to make the Championship 4. In addition, Barker was suspended for the [[2024 NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race|Phoenix finale]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/11/05/nascar-issues-major-penalties-to-three-cup-series-teams-after-martinsville/ |title=NASCAR issues major penalties to three Cup Series teams after Martinsville |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=November 5, 2024 |access-date=November 6, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260803222432/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/11/05/nascar-issues-major-penalties-to-three-cup-series-teams-after-martinsville/ |archive-date=August 3, 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Wallace started the [[2025 NASCAR Cup Series|2025 season]] with a 29th place DNF at the [[2025 Daytona 500]]. He won at [[2025 Brickyard 400|Indianapolis]] to break a 100-race winless streak and make the playoffs.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2025/07/25/results-cup-series-2025-indianapolis-brickyard-400-race-recap |title=Bubba Wallace holds on in double overtime, rolls to Brickyard 400 victory |first=Reid |last=Spencer |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=July 27, 2025 |access-date=July 28, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250804000204/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2025/07/25/results-cup-series-2025-indianapolis-brickyard-400-race-recap/ |archive-date=August 4, 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref>

===Car No. 35 history===
;Riley Herbst (2025–2026)
[[File:Riley Herbst 35 Sonoma 2025.jpg|thumb|[[Riley Herbst]] in the No. 35 car at [[Sonoma Raceway]] in 2025]]
On November 20, 2024, 23XI announced that [[Riley Herbst]] would drive the No. 35 for the [[2025 NASCAR Cup Series|2025 season]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2024/11/20/23xi-racing-expanding-to-three-teams-with-riley-herbst/ |title=23XI Racing expanding to three teams with Riley Herbst |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=November 20, 2024 |access-date=November 21, 2024}}</ref> The No. 35 was chosen by 23XI as an homage to Jordan's [[Minor League Baseball]] career.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://jordansdaily.com/2016/08/08/mjmondays-10/ |title=#MJMondays: MJ Suits Up for the Scottsdale Scorpions |first=Yale |last=Schalk |website=Jordans Daily |date=August 8, 2016 |access-date=November 23, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251212163535/https://jordansdaily.com/2016/08/08/mjmondays-10/ |archive-date=December 12, 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref> He started the season with a seventeenth-place at the [[2025 Daytona 500]]. Herbst struggled throughout the season, failing to score a single top-ten finish and only finishing inside the top-twenty-eight times with a best finish of fourteenth at [[2025 Würth 400|Texas]], and finished 35th in the final standings. Shortly after the [[2025 Bank of America Roval 400|Charlotte Roval race]], the No. 35 car was disqualified for failing post-race height inspection.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2025/10/05/charlotte-roval-post-race-inspection |title=Charlotte ROVAL post-race inspection |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=October 5, 2025 |access-date=October 6, 2025}}</ref>

Herbst started the [[2026 NASCAR Cup Series|2026 season]] with an eighth-place finish at the [[2026 Daytona 500]]. On May 30, 2026, it was announced that Corey Heim would be replacing Riley Herbst<ref>{{cite web |date=May 30, 2026 |title= Corey Heim to compete full time for 23XI Racing in 2027 |url= https://www.jayski.com/2026/05/30/corey-heim-to-compete-full-time-for-23xi-racing-in-2027/ |access-date=June 3, 2026 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media}}</ref> but would be staying in the No. 67 starting with the 2027 season. Herbst will move to [[Legacy Motor Club]]'s No. 84 starting in 2027.

On October 15, 2022, 23XI Racing announced that it bought out the remainder of [[Tyler Reddick]]'s contract from [[Richard Childress Racing]] to replace Kurt Busch in the No. 45 for the [[2023 NASCAR Cup Series|2023 season]]. Reddick originally signed with 23XI for 2024, but Busch's concussion and subsequent retirement from full-time competition led to this move.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2022/10/15/tyler-reddick-to-join-23xi-racing-in-2023-one-year-earlier-than-planned/ |title=Tyler Reddick to join 23XI Racing in 2023, one year earlier than planned |website=NASCAR.com |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media, LLC. |date=October 15, 2022 |access-date=October 16, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221015211617/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2022/10/15/tyler-reddick-to-join-23xi-racing-in-2023-one-year-earlier-than-planned/ |archive-date=October 15, 2022 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Reddick started the 2023 season with a 39th place DNF at the [[2023 Daytona 500]]. A month later, he scored his first win of the season at [[2023 EchoPark Automotive Grand Prix|COTA]] in triple overtime.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/03/26/2023-nascar-cup-series-circuit-of-the-americas-recap/ |title=Tyler Reddick scores first 2023 win at COTA in triple overtime |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=March 26, 2023 |access-date=March 27, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230227221310/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/02/26/results-cup-series-2023-auto-club-speedway-race-recap/ |archive-date=February 27, 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref> On May 16, NASCAR docked the No. 45 team ten owner and driver points for unapproved ballasts during inspection prior to qualifying at [[2023 Goodyear 400|Darlington]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2023/05/16/darlington-penalties-tyler-reddick-docked-10-points-no-43-team-has-two-crew-members-suspended/ |title=Darlington Penalties: Tyler Reddick docked 10 points; No. 43 team has two crew members suspended |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=May 16, 2023 |access-date=May 17, 2023}}</ref> During the playoffs, Reddick won at [[2023 Hollywood Casino 400|Kansas]] to advance to the Round of 12.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2023/09/10/results-cup-series-playoffs-2023-kansas-speedway-race-recap/ |title=Tyler Reddick cashes in at Kansas, advances in Cup Series Playoffs |first=Reid |last=Spencer |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=September 10, 2023 |access-date=September 11, 2023}}</ref>

Reddick started the [[2024 NASCAR Cup Series|2024 season]] with a 29th place DNF at the [[2024 Daytona 500]]. He scored his first win of the season at [[2024 GEICO 500|Talladega]] by overtaking [[Brad Keselowski]] on the final stretch after Keselowski spun [[Michael McDowell (racing driver)|Michael McDowell]] and triggered a multi-car crash.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/04/21/tyler-reddick-wins-cup-series-race-at-talladega/ |title=Tyler Reddick steals Talladega win as last-lap mayhem unfolds |first=Holly |last=Cain |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=April 21, 2024 |access-date=April 22, 2024}}</ref> He scored his second win of the season at [[2024 FireKeepers Casino 400|Michigan]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/08/19/results-cup-series-2024-michigan-race-recap/ |title=Tyler Reddick fends off Byron in second overtime, rolls to Michigan win |first=Holly |last=Cain |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=August 19, 2024 |access-date=August 20, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240819195423/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/08/19/results-cup-series-2024-michigan-race-recap/ |archive-date=August 19, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref> Following the [[2024 Cook Out Southern 500|Southern 500]], Reddick clinched the regular season championship, beating [[Kyle Larson]] by just one point.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/09/01/tyler-reddick-clinches-2024-regular-season-championship/ |title=Tyler Reddick clinches 2024 Regular Season Championship |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=September 1, 2024 |access-date=September 3, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240903091454/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/09/01/tyler-reddick-clinches-2024-regular-season-championship/ |archive-date=September 3, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref> During the playoffs, he locked himself into the Championship 4 by winning at [[2024 Straight Talk Wireless 400|Homestead]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/10/27/nascar-cup-series-2024-homestead-miami-playoff-race-recap/ |title=Tyler Reddick rides high line to Homestead victory, clinches Championship 4 berth |first=Holly |last=Cain |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=October 27, 2024 |access-date=October 28, 2024}}</ref>

Reddick started the [[2025 NASCAR Cup Series|2025 season]] with a runner-up finish at the [[2025 Daytona 500]]. Despite having no wins, he stayed consistent enough to make the playoffs until he was eliminated after the Round of 12, finishing ninth in the points standings.

Reddick began the [[2026 NASCAR Cup Series|2026 season]] by winning the [[2026 Daytona 500]], as well as [[2026 Autotrader 400|Atlanta]] and [[2026 DuraMAX Texas Grand Prix|COTA]], becoming the first driver in Cup Series history to win the first three races in a season.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/02/15/nascar-cup-series-daytona-500-2026-race-recap/ |title=A slam-dunk win! Tyler Reddick triumphs in 68th running of Daytona 500 |first=Reid |last=Spencer |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=February 15, 2026 |access-date=February 16, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260216003811/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/02/15/nascar-cup-series-daytona-500-2026-race-recap/ |archive-date=February 16, 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/03/01/nascar-cup-series-2026-tyler-reddick-circuit-of-the-americas-analysis/ |title=Analysis: Reddick takes mantle as NASCAR's best, delivering historic three-peat to start 2026 |first=Cameron |last=Richardson |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=March 1, 2026 |access-date=March 3, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260302163547/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/03/01/nascar-cup-series-2026-tyler-reddick-circuit-of-the-americas-analysis/ |archive-date=March 2, 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> In addition, Reddick won at [[2026 Goodyear 400|Darlington]] and [[2026 AdventHealth 400|Kansas]], becoming the first driver since [[Dale Earnhardt]] in [[1987 NASCAR Winston Cup Series|1987]] to score five of the first nine races in a season.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/04/19/cup-series-2026-kansas-spring-race-recap-results/ |title=Reddick prevails in overtime at Kansas for fifth Cup Series win of 2026 |first=Reid |last=Spencer |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=April 19, 2026 |access-date=April 20, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260419193045/https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/04/19/cup-series-2026-kansas-spring-race-recap-results/ |archive-date=April 19, 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> After having no victories for the rest of the regular season, he won at [[2026 Freeway Insurance 500|Phoenix]] during "The Chase".

===Car No. 50 history===
[[file:Corey Heim 50 Nashville.jpg|thumb|left|Heim at the [[2024 Ally 400]].]]
On March 13, 2024, it was announced that Kobayashi would once again compete in a race for the team, this time at [[2024 EchoPark Automotive Grand Prix|COTA]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://tobychristie.com/nascar/cup-series/kamui-kobayashi-returning-to-23xi-racing-for-cup-series-race-at-cota/ |title=Kamui Kobayashi Returning to 23XI Racing for Cup Series Race at COTA |last=Srigley |first=Joseph |website=TobyChristie.com |date=March 13, 2024 |access-date=March 13, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240313161836/https://tobychristie.com/nascar/cup-series/kamui-kobayashi-returning-to-23xi-racing-for-cup-series-race-at-cota/ |archive-date=March 13, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref> The car number would race as the No. 50 instead of the No. 67 to celebrate the 50th anniversary of sponsor [[Mobil 1]]. The team planned to run the No. 50 for two more races during the season with different drivers.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.jayski.com/2024/03/16/kamui-kobayashi-to-make-second-start-for-23xi-racing-at-circuit-of-the-americas/ |title=Kamui Kobayashi to make second start for 23XI Racing at Circuit of the Americas UPDATE |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media |date=March 16, 2024 |orig-date=March 13, 2024 |access-date=March 16, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240316161054/https://www.jayski.com/2024/03/16/kamui-kobayashi-to-make-second-start-for-23xi-racing-at-circuit-of-the-americas/ |archive-date=March 16, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref>

On May 10, 2024, 23XI Racing announced that Corey Heim would drive the No. 50 Mobil 1 Toyota Camry at Nashville Superspeedway.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.23xiracing.com/post/23xi-racing-to-field-a-third-entry-for-corey-heim-at-nashville | title=23XI Racing to Field a Third Entry for Corey Heim at Nashville | access-date=2024-09-03 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240903184838/https://www.23xiracing.com/post/23xi-racing-to-field-a-third-entry-for-corey-heim-at-nashville | archive-date=2024-09-03 | url-status=live }}</ref> Heim finished 29th after crashing out in one of five overtimes.

On August 2, 2024, the team announced that [[Juan Pablo Montoya]] would make his NASCAR Cup Series return for 23XI at Watkins Glen on September 15.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2024/09/03/five-thought-starters-that-prove-the-2024-nascar-playoffs-will-be-a-rare-breed/ | title=Five thought starters that prove the 2024 NASCAR Playoffs will be a rare breed | date=3 September 2024 }}</ref> This would be the 3rd and final start for the No. 50 Mobil 1 entry in 2024. Montoya finished 32nd in his return to stock car racing.
