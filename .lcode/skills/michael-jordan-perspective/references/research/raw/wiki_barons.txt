The '''Birmingham Barons''' are a [[Minor League Baseball]] team based in [[Birmingham, Alabama]]. The team, which plays in the [[Southern League (1964–present)|Southern League]], is the [[Double-A (baseball)|Double-A]] affiliate of the [[Chicago White Sox]] and plays at [[Regions Field]] in downtown Birmingham. The Barons were previously located in [[Montgomery, Alabama]], and known as the [[Montgomery Rebels]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://funwhileitlasted.net/2017/11/18/1965-1980-montgomery-rebels/|title=1965-1980 Montgomery Rebels - Fun While It Lasted|date=2017-11-18|work=Fun While It Lasted|access-date=2018-04-24|language=en-US |df=mdy-all}}</ref>

==History==
Most of the professional baseball teams that have played in Birmingham have used the name Barons. The current team began playing in Birmingham in 1981, having previously played in [[Montgomery, Alabama]] as the [[Montgomery Rebels]]. Like previous Barons teams, they played at [[Rickwood Field]] on a full-time basis from 1981 to 1987.  Since then, they have only played at Rickwood on special occasions. From 1988 to 2012, the team played at [[Hoover Metropolitan Stadium]] in the suburb of [[Hoover, Alabama]], where former basketball star [[Michael Jordan]] played with the team in {{baseball year|1994}}.<ref>{{cite web|last=Crenshaw, Jr.|first=Solomon|title=20 years ago today, Michael Jordan signed with White Sox to produce our Summer of Jordan|url=https://www.al.com/sports/2014/02/20_years_ago_today_michael_jor.html|website=AL.com Alabama News|date=February 7, 2014|access-date=January 23, 2025}}</ref> Since 2013, the team has played at [[Regions Field]] in the downtown part of Birmingham.

As a result of [[Major League Baseball]]'s restructuring of Minor League Baseball in 2021, the Barons were organized into the eight-team Double-A South.<ref>{{cite web|last=Mayo|first=Jonathan|title=MLB Announces New Minors Teams, Leagues|url=https://www.mlb.com/news/new-minor-league-baseball-structure|website=Major League Baseball|date=February 12, 2021|access-date=June 18, 2024}}</ref> In 2022, the Double-A South became known as the Southern League, the name historically used by the regional circuit prior to the 2021 reorganization.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.milb.com/news/minor-league-baseball-historical-league-names-to-return-in-2022|title=Historical League Names to Return in 2022|website=Minor League Baseball|date=March 16, 2022|access-date=June 18, 2024}}</ref>

[[Elmore Sports Group]] purchased the team in 1995.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |title=Team History |url=https://www.milb.com/news/gcs-343755 |access-date=2026-01-17 |website=MiLB.com |language=en}}</ref> In 2005, [[Elmore Sports Group]] sold to team to [[Don Logan]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2005-09-29 |title=Barons welcome new ownership |url=https://www.oursportscentral.com/services/releases/barons-welcome-new-ownership/n-3227062 |access-date=2026-01-17 |website=OurSports Central |language=en}}</ref> In 2023, [[Diamond Baseball Holdings]] purchased the team from [[Don Logan|The Logan Family]].<ref name="sale2023" />

==Television and radio==
All Birmingham Barons games are televised live on [[MiLB.TV]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.milb.com/milb/multimedia/index.jsp?affiliateID=MiLBmenu |title=MiLB.tv |publisher=Minor League Baseball |access-date=July 28, 2017}}</ref> All games are also broadcast on radio on either [[WJQX]] 100.5 FM, [[WJOX-FM]] 94.5 FM or [[WJOX (AM)|WJOX-AM]] 690 AM.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.milb.com/documents/7/6/6/180554766/2016_Media_Guide_Final_JOB2_e29v6yel.pdf |title=Covering the Barons |work=Birmingham Barons Media Guide |publisher=Minor League Baseball |access-date=July 28, 2017 |page=108}}</ref> Birmingham Barons Hall-of-Fame broadcaster Curt Bloom is the broadcast commentator for both WERC and MiLB.TV and has been the voice of the Barons since 1992.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.milb.com/documents/7/6/6/180554766/2016_Media_Guide_Final_JOB2_e29v6yel.pdf |title=Barons Front Office Staff |work=Birmingham Barons Media Guide |publisher=Minor League Baseball |access-date=July 28, 2017 |page=105}}</ref>
